### 🔬 Research Interests
Trustworthy deep learning for medical image analysis, with a focus on understanding when and why models fail:
* Label-free quality estimation and failure detection for segmentation
* Overconfidence in medical models
* Robustness and rare-class recall of compressed models
* Cross-dataset generalization and shortcut learning

Methodologically, self-supervised representation learning is used to build reliable models under scarce labels; explainable AI is a secondary interest.
